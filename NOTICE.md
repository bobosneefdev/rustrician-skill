This project is an unofficial companion to rustrician.io. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by
rustrician.io, JaviteSoft, the Rustricity Workshop or Facepunch Studios.

Most of `skills/rustrician/` is generated from other people's work. Their rights are theirs.

- **[rustrician.io](https://rustrician.io/)** by **JaviteSoft**: the electricity simulator. Component names,
  descriptions, tips, ports and properties in `skills/rustrician/assets/catalog.json` and
  `skills/rustrician/references/components/` come from the simulator's published scripts. The item list in
  `skills/rustrician/assets/items.json` comes from the simulator's item endpoint.
- **[The Rust Electrical Handbook](https://rustrician.io/handbook/)**, created and maintained by **@SwiftCoyote**
  with contributions from the **[Rustricity Workshop](https://discord.rustrician.io/)** community: the text in
  `skills/rustrician/references/guides/`, the handbook sections of `skills/rustrician/references/components/`,
  `skills/rustrician/references/handbook-items-not-in-simulator.md` and the circuit list in
  `skills/rustrician/references/example-circuits.md`. Circuits linked there belong to their authors.
- **Rust** is a game by Facepunch Studios.

To correct or improve that content, contribute to the handbook through the Rustricity Workshop Discord. This
repository picks up the change automatically. If you are a rights holder and want content removed or
attributed differently, open an issue.
